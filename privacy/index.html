Privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

In short: disappear collects no personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on your phone.

Who is responsible

The game disappear is made by Ramin Faghihi (FAVR Studio), who is responsible for this game and this website. Contact: disappear.mobilegame@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, stars, settings, Style choices and Daily results are saved only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the game deletes them.

No internet connection, no third parties

The game does not connect to the internet while you play. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software.

Sharing a maze

When you tap Copy, WhatsApp, Message or Email on the share screen, the game copies the maze code and opens the app you picked. What you send there is handled by that app and its provider, not by us.

App stores and beta tests

Apple (App Store, TestFlight) and Google (Google Play) process data when you download or test the game, under their own privacy policies. If you join a beta test, Apple or Google pass on to us your email address if we invited you by email, crash reports, and any feedback and screenshots you choose to send. We use these only to fix and improve the game and delete them when they are no longer needed for that, or when you ask. The stores also show us anonymous statistics, such as the number of downloads and crashes.

Email

If you write to us, we use your address and message only to answer you.

Children

The game collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to complain to a data protection authority. Since the game stores nothing with us, this mainly concerns emails and beta feedback. Write to disappear.mobilegame@gmail.com.

This website

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement. The pages use no cookies and no analytics.

Changes

If the game ever starts collecting data, for example through purchases or ads, this page will say so before that version is released.
