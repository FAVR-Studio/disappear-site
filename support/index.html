disappear

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: write to disappear.mobilegame@gmail.com. Please tell us your phone model and the game version (shown on the Pause screen).

How to play

Each level shows the maze for a few seconds. Then the walls disappear. Drag anywhere on the screen to move the pear to the exit. If you bump into a wall, it shows for a moment. Hold Peek to see the whole maze again; it costs a little time. Finish without Peek and without bumping for all three stars.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your phone. There is no account and no cloud sync, so deleting the game deletes your progress. A new phone starts fresh.

How do I share a maze?

In the Lab, make a maze and tap Share. Your friend opens disappear, goes to Lab, then Enter code, and types or pastes the code.

Testing the beta

On iPhone, use Send Beta Feedback in the TestFlight app, or take a screenshot while playing. On Android, write to us by email. Crashes, freezes and anything that felt unfair help most.
